# How to Update Your Website

**Timberline Custom Homes**

You can update your portfolio, photos, testimonials, FAQs, team and contact details yourself. You can't break the design: you only change the words and pictures, and the website keeps its look automatically.

---

## Logging in

Go to **https://timberlinecustomhomes.ca/studio** (bookmark it) and sign in with the email you were invited with.

The menu on the left:

| Menu item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| 🏡 **Portfolio** | Your projects and categories |
| 💬 **Testimonials** | Client reviews |
| ❓ **FAQs** | Questions at the bottom of every page |
| 👷 **Team Members** | The team on the About page |
| 🖼️ **Page Banners** | The big photo and title at the top of each page |
| ⚙️ **Contact Details & Home Slider** | Phone, email, address, social links, home page photos |

---

## The golden rule: click Publish

Changes are saved as a **draft** while you type, but they only go on the website once you click the green **Publish** button (bottom right).

**The website updates about 2–3 minutes after you publish.** Refresh the website to see it.

---

## Add a new project

1. Click **Portfolio → Projects by Category** → pick the category (for example **Boathouses**).
2. Click the **✏️ / +** button at the top to create a new project.
3. Fill in:
   - **Project name**, for example *Sunset Glory Boathouse*
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**
   - **Category** (already filled in)
   - **Location**, for example *Stoney Lake, ON*
   - **Description**: 1–2 sentences
   - **Cover photo**: the main photo
   - **Photo gallery**: drag and drop all the photos at once, then drag them into the order you like
4. Click **Publish**.

### Renovations & Additions (Before & After)

For projects in **Renovations & Additions**, you'll see **Before & After photos** instead of a gallery:

1. Click **Add item**.
2. Upload the **BEFORE** photo and the **AFTER** photo of the same spot.
3. Add a short caption, for example *Kitchen: new cabinetry and lake view*.
4. Repeat for each room or angle, then click **Publish**.

On the website, visitors drag a slider to compare before and after.

---

## Change or remove a project

- **Edit:** open the project, make your changes, then click **Publish**.
- **Replace a photo:** hover over the photo → **⋯** → **Replace** or **Remove**.
- **Remove a project:** open it → **⋯** (next to Publish) → **Delete** (or **Unpublish** to hide it but keep it).
- **Change the order:** go to **Portfolio → All Projects (drag to reorder)** and drag projects up or down. The order is saved straight away.

---

## Testimonials

- Add one with the **+** button: paste the quote, client name and location/year, and optionally a project photo.
- Turn on **"Feature on the Home and About pages"** for your 2–3 favourites (they show a ★). All testimonials always appear on the Testimonials page.
- Drag to change the order.

## FAQs

Add, edit or reorder questions. To start a new paragraph in an answer, **leave an empty line**.

## Team Members

Add or edit names, job titles and start years, choose **Office** or **Field** team, and drag to reorder.

## Page Banners

Choose a page (tabs along the top), then change the photo, title or subtitle. If you leave a field empty, the website keeps the current text.

## Contact Details & Home Slider

Update the phone, email, address and social media links. They change everywhere on the website at once. The **Home page slider** tab holds the big photos on the home page; drag to reorder.

---

## Photo tips

- **Landscape** photos look best. Use at least **2000 pixels wide** for banners and the home slider.
- Any size is fine to upload: the website automatically shrinks photos so they load fast.
- Use your **best photo as the cover**. It's what people see first.

## Something not showing?

1. Did you click **Publish**? (Unpublished drafts show an orange dot.)
2. Wait 3 minutes, then refresh the website (press **Ctrl + F5** on a computer).
3. Still not there? Click **🚀 Update Website** at the top of the editor → **Update website now**.
